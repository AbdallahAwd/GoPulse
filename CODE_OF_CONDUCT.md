# Code of conduct

GoPulse welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be
respectful, assume good intent, and make technical criticism specific to the work.
Explain decisions, accept corrections and give credit for others' contributions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
spam and publishing someone's private information are unacceptable in project
issues, pull requests and other spaces maintained by this project.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions or
restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and
prior history, and protect affected people's privacy.

For a conduct concern, contact a repository maintainer using a private channel
listed on their GitHub profile. If none is available, open a minimal issue
requesting private contact without naming affected people or including sensitive
details. Maintainers must not require public disclosure to review a report.
